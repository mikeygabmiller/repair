## Recalls that may apply to this car

OFFICIAL RECORD: NHTSA (`recalls.md`, 24 campaigns for all 2005 Accords). Whether each
applies, and whether it's already been done, is **only** known by entering the VIN at
<https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls>. Each recall notice says the fix is free at a Honda
dealer. Confirm that when booking, because the free-repair obligation depends on the
car's age when the recall was issued.

**Do first: Takata airbag inflators.** Campaigns 19V499000, 19V501000, 19V182000,
15V370000, 15V320000, 14V700000, 14V353000 (the last two were regional: humid areas and
California). Inflators can explode in a crash and throw metal fragments. 19V182000,
19V499000 and 19V501000 cover cars that *already* got a replacement inflator, so "it was
done once" doesn't mean it's finished.

Other airbag campaigns: 18V268000 (passenger airbag installed wrong during a
replacement), 16V178000 (wrong passenger airbag module at the factory, cars built
Oct 1, 2003 – Aug 17, 2007), 04V551000 (driver airbag fabric could tear).

Others that cover this model:
- **11V395000, automatic transmission software**: 2005–2010 Accord built from
  July 1, 2004. A broken secondary-shaft bearing race can cause noise and a check-engine
  light. Fix: free transmission control software update.
- **07V097000, fuel pump relay**: relay coil wire may break, engine won't start. Fix:
  relay inspected/replaced.
- **05V132000, main fuse box terminal**: loose terminal can cut fuel pump power. Fix:
  fuse box replaced.
- **07V001000, airbag seat position sensor wiring**: wire may break and turn on the
  airbag light. Fix: sub-harness replaced.

Don't apply unless the car has that part:
- **12V222000** (power steering hose): **V6 only**.
- **19E068000** (accelerator pedal): only aftermarket Dorman pedals.
- **Headlight/lighting "E" campaigns** (09E025000, 09E012000, 07E031000, 07E004000,
  06E064000, 06E038000, 06E032000, 06E023000): only aftermarket lights.

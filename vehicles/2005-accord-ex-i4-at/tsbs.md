What this is: NHTSA's one-line summary of every communication Honda filed that names the 2005 Accord (all engines, sedan and coupe; hybrid rows are left out). **The summary is NHTSA's, not the bulletin's text.** It tells you a bulletin exists and what it is about; the actual fix, part numbers and VIN ranges are only in the bulletin PDF, free on nhtsa.gov: search the car, open "Manufacturer Communications", find the NHTSA ID. Many bulletins are V6-, coupe- or other-year-only even though NHTSA lists them here.

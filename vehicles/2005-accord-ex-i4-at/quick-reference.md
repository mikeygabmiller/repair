# Quick reference: 2005 Accord EX-L sedan, 2.4 L, automatic

One page for the things looked up most: fluids, tires, fuses, bulbs, roadside. Every row is
from the owner's manual pages Claude read on 2026-11-10 (page images), or copied from
`specs.md` where it says so. "OM p.N" = printed page; in the PDF it is page N+1. The fuse
box lids and the door-jamb label on the car outrank the manual (Tier 1).

## Fuses

Interior box: driver's lower left of the dash; pull the lid toward you. Under-hood box:
back of the engine bay, driver's side; push the tabs. Fuse puller is in the under-hood box.
Replace with the **same rating or lower, never higher**. A new fuse that blows again soon
= a real fault: leave it and diagnose (VERIFIED: OM pp.265–267).

The manual marks some rows as 6-cylinder or Canada only with a symbol that did not survive
in this PDF; amps printed in brackets are the rows the manual sets apart. Where a number
has two rows, the lid of the fuse box on the car says which one this car has.

## Roadside (full steps: `procedures/accord-2005-roadside.md`)

| Situation | The short version | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Jump start | + to + first; then booster − to the **grounding strap** on this engine, not the battery − and not anywhere else on the engine. Remove in reverse. Don't jump a frozen battery. Can't push-start an automatic | VERIFIED: OM pp.253–255 |
| Overheating | Pull over, A/C off. Steam = engine off, wait. No steam = idle and watch the gauge. Never open the radiator cap hot | VERIFIED: OM pp.256–257 |
| Oil pressure light on while running | Stop and shut off **immediately**. Check level after a minute, top up, restart: light must go out within 10 s or it gets towed | VERIFIED: OM p.258 |
| Charging light | Everything electrical off, don't shut the engine off, drive straight to help | VERIFIED: OM p.259 |
| Check engine light after fuelling | Tighten the fuel cap; the light takes at least three days of normal driving to clear | VERIFIED: OM p.260 |
| BRAKE light while driving | Probably low fluid. Pedal normal: check the level soon. Pedal low or soft: stop, tow | VERIFIED: OM p.261 |
| Towing | Flatbed is best; wheel-lift is acceptable. Front wheels on the ground only if unavoidable: engine running, shift to D then N, engine off, parking brake off; max 50 miles at under 35 mph. Never by the bumpers | VERIFIED: OM pp.263–264 |
| Moonroof stuck open | Check the fuse, then close it by hand: wrench from the trunk tool kit, round plug in the headliner | VERIFIED: OM p.262 |
| Readiness codes (emissions test) | After a battery disconnect they take at least three days of mixed driving. Key ON, engine off: the check engine light stays on 20 s then goes out = set; blinks 5 times = not set | VERIFIED: OM p.260 |

## Battery (VERIFIED: OM pp.242–243)

Look at the test indicator window monthly. Corrosion: baking soda and water, rinse, dry,
coat the terminals with grease. Disconnect **negative first, reconnect it last**. Disconnect
both cables before charging. Wash hands after (lead). Rating 12 V, 38 AH/5 HR (OM p.275).

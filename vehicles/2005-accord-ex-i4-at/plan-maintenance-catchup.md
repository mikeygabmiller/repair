# Plan: catching up the maintenance nobody recorded

Written 2026-10-25 from `open-issues.md` item 6 and `specs.md`. Every type and quantity
here is copied from `specs.md`, which cites the owner's manual page; nothing is new.
Assume the **severe** schedule applies (short local trips, de-iced winter roads: OM p.199,
`specs.md`).

Do these **after** the right front job (`plan-right-front-2026-10.md`), never on the same
weekend, and none of them the night before a work day.

## Order, and why

| # | Job | Why this order | DIY? | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Oil and filter**, and write down the mileage | Cheapest, last change unknown, and it starts the log | Yes | ~1 h |
| 2 | **Brake fluid** | Every 3 years regardless of mileage; no record. The right front brake will already be opened up in the axle job, so ask that shop for a price on a flush while it's there | Shop is simplest (needs bleeding at all four wheels; a two-person job DIY) | Shop: ~1 h |
| 3 | **ATF drain and refill** | No record, and old ATF can cause a shudder that feels like a CV axle (`open-issues.md` item 2, UNVERIFIED). Do it before blaming the mounts if the shake survives the new axle | Yes | ~1 h per drain-and-refill |
| 4 | **Engine air filter + cabin filter** | Two-minute jobs, no tools or a screwdriver | Yes | 15 min |
| 5 | **Inspect the drive belt** | Every 30k / 2 yr. Look for cracks and glazing | Yes, look only | 10 min |
| 6 | **Coolant** | 120k / 10 yr first change; the car is past both. Not urgent if the level and colour are right, but it's due | Yes, or shop | ~1.5 h |
| 7 | Valve clearance | Inspect at 110k, otherwise only if noisy. Listen first | Shop | — |

(OM p.219). Don't read it cold and overfill.

## Free things to do first

- Ask a Honda parts counter (VIN 1HGCM56895A072328) for: the oil filter, the ATF drain
  plug washer, whether DW-1 replaces Z1, and the air and cabin filters. Five part numbers
  in one call; update `parts.md` with the answers.
- Check the coolant reserve level and colour, and the brake fluid level and colour, at
  the next fill-up. Write down what you see.

## What I couldn't verify

- Every part number marked UNVERIFIED above (from past chats): a Honda parts counter
  with the VIN, free.
- How much brake fluid a full flush uses: the factory service manual (Chilton, free with
  a library card), or the shop.
- That old ATF can feel like a bad axle: a common forum claim, not checked against a
  Honda bulletin. NHTSA's free bulletin list for this car (nhtsa.gov, Manufacturer
  Communications) is where to look.

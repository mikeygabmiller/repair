# Roadmap to 500,000 miles: 2005 Accord EX-L 2.4 L automatic

Written 2026-11-17. Two parts: the **scheduled** work Honda lists (generated, exact counts),
and the **unscheduled** big parts that wear out with no Honda interval (judgment, no
mileage promised). Keep-or-sell decisions on any big item still go through the walk-away
worksheet in `long-term-ownership.md`.

**Pace assumed: 10,000 mi a year** (`long-term-ownership.md`: "about 10,000 mi a year",
rough). If the real pace differs, change the number in the marker line below and run
`python3 tools/due.py roadmap --write`. The ledger's odometer readings will show the
real pace once a few more are logged.

## Scheduled work (Honda severe schedule, OM p.203)

Counts are how many times each item comes due in each mileage band, from the newest
odometer reading in `ledger.csv` and the intervals in `schedule.csv`. "Now" items (no
record) land in the first band. The time limit counts too: at 10,000 mi a year, "6 months"
means an oil change every 5,000 mi anyway, but brake fluid's 3 years is what drives it.

## Unscheduled: the big parts that wear out

No Honda interval exists for any of these, so no mileage is promised here. "Replace on
failure, safety-critical parts OEM or name-brand" (`long-term-ownership.md`). What to watch
for comes first, because catching it early is what keeps it cheap.

| Part | Watch for (free) | Cost on file | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Automatic transmission** (most likely thing to end the car) | Slipping, flare between gears, harsh or late shifts, burnt-smelling ATF (`symptom-index.md`) | None. Transmission fund in `long-term-ownership.md` | COMMUNITY: widely seen as this generation's weak point; UNVERIFIED |

## How to keep this current

- Every finished job gets a row in `ledger.csv` (the checklists' log box writes it for
  you), so this table always starts from the real odometer.
- Run `python3 tools/due.py roadmap --write` after adding ledger rows; `tools/check.py`
  warns when the table is stale.
- When a shop quotes one of the unscheduled parts, put the quote in its row with the date.

## What I couldn't verify

- **The 10,000 mi/yr pace**: rough, from about three months of ownership. Check it against
  the ledger once a year.
- **The automatic as the weak point, the rust spots, timing chain and VTC wear**:
  COMMUNITY. Owner complaints (`complaints.md`) show where failures cluster, never when.
- **Part prices**: HondaPartsNow listings from 2026-11-03, before shipping. Not checked by
  VIN at a Honda counter (`owner-checklist.md`, call A).

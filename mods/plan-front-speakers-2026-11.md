## The principle that decides "louder"

With a small head-unit amp, the speaker's **sensitivity** (dB at 1 W / 1 m, on the spec
sheet) decides loudness more than its watt rating. Every +3 dB of sensitivity is about
the same loudness gain as doubling amp power. Ignore "300 W peak" on the box; compare
sensitivity. Big gains past that need an amp (Eonon A304 or a 4-channel amp), which is
a separate job.

Deadening helps clarity and mid-bass: the bare door skin rings and cancels the speaker's
back wave. Butyl mat adds mass so the panel stops vibrating. Partial coverage of the outer
skin behind the speaker does most of the work; sealing the large inner access holes turns
the door into a better enclosure (COMMUNITY: general car-audio practice).

# Install Labor Costs for Dash-Switch Car Electrical Accessories (2025-2026, US with WA / Puget Sound focus)

Researched 2026-10-26. Scope: labor rates and published flat-rate install prices that can be used to give each of ~50 blank-switch-slot accessory ideas for the 2005 Honda Accord EX (2.4 L, automatic) an "average install cost" (parts + shop labor) and a DIY time estimate.

Source quality note: Best Buy's own service-category page is the strongest source of published flat-rate prices (first-party, current). Labor-rate surveys come from PartsTech (via Ratchet+Wrench and AAA). Washington-specific data was thin: no Seattle/Tacoma shop published a price list I could find, and no Seattle Honda dealer publishes its customer labor rate online. Figures labeled ESTIMATE are my inference, not sourced numbers.

## Typical hourly labor rates (general repair, Honda dealer, 12-volt / car-audio shops), WA and national

### Takeaway
Nationally, the most common general-shop rate is $100-$119/hr, and about half of shops charge $100-$139/hr (PartsTech survey of about 700 shops, Sept 2025). Dealers typically run 25%+ higher. No trustworthy Seattle-specific posted rate was found. AAA's page lists Washington at $249.87/hr, but that figure comes from World Population Review with no stated method, and it looks inflated next to the survey data. 12-volt shops mostly quote flat rates per job, not hourly rates.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- ESTIMATE for Puget Sound planning: independent general shop about $150-$200/hr; Honda dealer about $190-$250/hr; 12-volt shop flat rates close to Best Buy's, often slightly higher. Basis: national PartsTech median of $100-$139, a high-cost metro premium, the AAA 25%+ dealer premium, and AAA's WA figure as a ceiling. The owner should confirm by calling 2-3 shops.
- For the accessory table, a blended planning rate of about $150/hr for an independent or 12-volt shop in Puget Sound is a reasonable ESTIMATE. Use about $200+/hr if a Honda dealer installs Honda accessories.

### Gaps
- No primary PartsTech report text, so the $100-$139 vs. $120-$159 conflict is unresolved.
- No published 2025-2026 customer labor rate for any Seattle/Tacoma Honda dealer or independent shop. Checkbook (paywalled; often free through King County Library) and phone calls are the way to get one.
- RepairPal shows job totals by city, not hourly rates, so I found no RepairPal Seattle hourly figure.
- No Crutchfield-referred installer rates were found.

### Inferences
These are planning ESTIMATES, not sourced prices. They take Best Buy flat rates where they exist, and otherwise sourced hours × about $150/hr (Puget Sound independent/12-volt shop, see above):
- USB/12V outlet added to a blank switch slot, fused add-a-circuit, simple run: about 0.5-1.0 hr shop labor (about $75-$150). No published flat rate was found.
- LED accent/footwell lighting on a switch: about 1-1.5 hr (about $150-$225).
- Power inverter hardwired to the battery with a fuse and a remote switch or relay: about 1-2 hr (about $150-$300). A firewall pass-through is needed. Use an existing grommet; never drill (owner rule).
- Auxiliary switch panel or relay box (e.g., a 4-6 circuit unit): about 1.5-3 hr ($225-$450), depending on the number of circuits.
- Kill switch: often sold as an add-on with an alarm or remote start. Standalone hidden kill switch about 1 hr ($150). The Best Buy security install is $229.99 for a full alarm.
- Remote battery disconnect (solenoid type): about 1-1.5 hr ($150-$225).
- Horn upgrade with a relay: about 1-1.5 hr ($150-$225). On this Accord, bumper or grille access may add time.
- Subwoofer + mono amp + bass knob: Best Buy $199.99 for the amp, plus a likely sub/box and amp-wiring-kit charge. Total labor about $200-$300.
- Fog lights on a 2003-2005 Accord sedan: front bumper cover removal is likely (the 2008-2010 Honda sedan fog kits require it), plus a switch and harness. Shop labor about 2-3 hr ($300-$450) plus parts.
- DIY time is typically about 1.5-2× the shop/book time for a first-timer (ESTIMATE, general rule; no source).
- Labor rarely leaves the car undrivable for long. Fog lights (bumper off) and seat heaters (seats out, SRS occupant sensor concerns on the front passenger seat) are the longest jobs.

### Gaps
- No published flat rates were found for: fog light install at Best Buy, power inverter hardwire, LED accent/footwell lighting, auxiliary switch panel, remote battery disconnect, horn upgrade, standalone kill switch, or standalone USB/12V outlet add. The estimates above fill these in.
- No Seattle/Tacoma 12-volt shop publishes prices online. Rainier Audio and Foss Audio & Tint need phone quotes.
- No Katzkin or Dorman seat-heater installer price list was found. The seat-heater figures rest on low-quality and forum sources only.

## Typical install hours (Honda accessory book time and common jobs)

### Takeaway
I could not find Honda's accessory installation instructions for the 2003-2005 Accord sedan fog lights. The later Honda sedan fog-light instructions I found (2008, 2009, 2010, 2017) list no install time. They do require front bumper removal and say the work is for skilled technicians, not DIYers. Published hour figures for dash cams, remote starts and similar jobs were not found. Hours have to be inferred from flat rates or forum reports.

### Cited Findings
- Honda dealer accessory instructions for the 2009 Accord 4-door fog lights (AII 40001, EX/EX-L) list parts and tools but no labor time. They state the work is for skilled technicians with proper tools and training and should not be attempted by do-it-yourselfers. — [Bernardi Parts PDF 2009](https://www.bernardiparts.com/Images/Install/2009_AccordSedan_Foglights_AII40001.pdf)
- The 2008 Accord sedan (L4) and 2010 Accord sedan Honda fog-light instructions describe removing the front bumper and mounting the lights with self-tapping screws plus a bracket. — [2008 AII35357](https://www.bernardiparts.com/Images/Install/2008_AccordSedan_Foglights(L4)_AII35357.pdf); [2010 AII42264](https://www.bernardiparts.com/Images/Install/2010_AccordSedan_Foglights_AII42264.pdf)
- 2017 Accord sedan fog-light instructions include relay bracket and ground wiring steps. — [2017 AII04162-18](https://www.bernardiparts.com/Images/Install/2017_AccordSedan_FogLights_AII04162-18.pdf)
- OEM 2003 Accord fog light assemblies are listed for both 2-door and 4-door. — [HondaPartsNow](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/oem-2003-honda-accord-fog_light.html)
- Self-tapping-screw mounting in the later kits implies new holes in the bumper area. Whether the 2003-2005 kit does the same is unconfirmed (see Gaps). This matters because of the owner's no-drilling rule. — [Bernardi Parts PDF 2008](https://www.bernardiparts.com/Images/Install/2008_AccordSedan_Foglights(L4)_AII35357.pdf)
- Fog-light forum anecdote: 2-3 hrs of labor for an HID fog setup (2009). — [Acurazine](https://acurazine.com/forums/1g-tsx-2004-2008-124/installation-costs-649283)

### Inferences
- ESTIMATE shop hours, derived by dividing Best Buy flat rates by about $100-$150/hr: dash cam front hardwire about 0.75-1 hr; front + rear about 1.5 hr; backup camera about 1-1.5 hr; remote start with T-harness about 1.5-2.5 hr; mono amp about 1.5-2 hr.
- A Honda 2003-2005 Accord fog kit likely runs 1.5-3 hr shop time (bumper removal plus harness and switch). This is UNVERIFIED until the 2003-2005 Honda installation instruction (AII number) is found. A Honda dealer parts counter can print it free when asked about the kit part number.

### Gaps
- The 2003-2005 Accord sedan Honda fog-light accessory instruction (and its AII number) was not found online. Its install time, and whether it requires drilling, are unknown.
- No free published book (labor-guide) times for accessory installs were found. Labor guides like Mitchell/ALLDATA are paywalled. A library ALLDATA/Chilton login may show Honda accessory-related labor times.

## Minimum / diagnostic charges

### Takeaway
Little published data. One 12-volt shop starts troubleshooting at $69.99. Best Buy's base flat rates exclude parts and nonstandard work and come with a quote before extra charges. A general-shop minimum is commonly about 0.5-1 hr of labor (ESTIMATE, unsourced).

### Cited Findings
- National Auto Sound & Security: troubleshooting from $69.99, free if under the shop's warranty. — [National Auto Sound](https://nationalautosound.com/car-audio-installation-remote-start-installation/)
- Best Buy: "Your Autotech will provide an estimate prior to performing work that requires an additional charge (including nonstandard installation) and any additional parts." Customers report separate charges for hardwire-kit parts. Remote start with T-harness carries "Additional materials fees." — [Best Buy listing](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/4265502.p); [Best Buy Q&A](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/questions/dash-camera-install/4265502)

### Inferences
- In the accessory table, add about $10-$40 for materials (wire, fuse taps, heat-shrink, loom) per job (ESTIMATE).
- At a Puget Sound general shop, expect a 1-hr diagnostic minimum, about $150-$200 (ESTIMATE, based on the rate inferences above).

### Gaps
- No Seattle-area published diagnostic fee or minimum labor charge was found.
- No Best Buy materials-fee dollar amounts were found.

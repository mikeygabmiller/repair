# Blank switch slots in the 2003-2007 Accord sedan (2005 EX I4 AT) and the genuine Honda switches that fit them

Scope: US 2005 Accord sedan EX, 2.4 L I4, 5AT (catalog code "4 Door EX KA 5AT"), VIN 1HGCM56895A072328. The owner's `profile.md` says interior "Type B", and HondaPartsNow's color key calls that "B-GRAY/BLACK". That match is an inference; check it against the door-jamb label.

Source types used below:
- **[OEM catalog]**: a Honda dealer parts catalog page (HondaPartsNow, HondaPartsConnection, Honda DreamShop). Tier 3.
- **[Honda install sheet]**: American Honda accessory installation instructions (AII 24050, AII 32672). These are factory documents but were hosted by third parties.
- **[Owner's manual]**: the repo copy at `sources/2005-accord-owners-manual.pdf`. Tier 1.
- **[Community]**: forums and eBay listings. Tips only, never specs.

## Where are the blank slots, how many are on an EX I4, and what fills them on other trims?

### Takeaway
The blanks are in a small switch bank in the driver's dashboard lower cover, to the left of the steering column, below the left side vent. Honda's catalog for the 2005 4-door EX KA 5AT lists **two** "Lid, Switch Hole" blanks plus the sunroof switch. The LX has three blanks. The EX-V6 fills one more slot with the TCS Off switch, leaving one blank. Heated-seat switches (EX-L and EX-V6) do **not** go in this bank; they sit in the center console beside the shifter. The fog light accessory switch is designed to go into one of these blanks.

Interior color shown: A-BLACK. — [HondaPartsNow 2005 4dr EX switch diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2005-honda-accord--4dr_ex-ka_5at/electrical_exhaust_heater_fuel/switch.html)

### Inferences
- The bank appears to have three positions. The lid count plus switch count is 3 on every trim checked: LX 3 lids; EX 2 lids + sunroof; EX-L 2 lids + sunroof; EX-V6 1 lid + sunroof + TCS. So the owner's EX I4 should have the moonroof switch and **two** blanks. This matches the 2005 owner's "3 spots" if that car had no moonroof.
- The only switches Honda put in this bank on US 2003-2007 sedans are the sunroof switch, the TCS Off switch (V6), and the accessory fog switch. VSA did not exist on these years; the "VSA" in the forum reply means TCS.
- The cruise "dummy" 36770-SDA-A51 is not a dash-slot blank. Its name suggests a steering-wheel or cruise switch position, not the dash bank.
- 2006-2007 EX-V6 sedans may differ (some had factory fogs and different trim). Their switch diagram was not checked.

### Gaps
- HondaPartsNow's 2005 EX diagram shows lid 77625-SDA-A01ZA only for interior "A-BLACK". The page did not show whether a 2005 car with B-GRAY/BLACK interior (likely the owner's Type B) uses the same lid or another color suffix. The 2007 diagram lists A01ZA for all three colors. Check by VIN at a dealer counter, or pull one lid and compare its color.
- I did not see a photo or drawing that shows which of the three positions the sunroof switch occupies.

## Honda fog light kit 08V31-SDA-101 (2003-2005 sedan): contents, status, used prices, bulb type, and the 2006-2007 kit

### Takeaway
08V31-SDA-101 replaces 08V31-SDA-100, fits 2003-2005 4-door (all trims, including the 2005 EX KA 5AT), and is **discontinued**. Honda's install sheet for the -100 kit lists a **switch harness and a switch**, plus the fog light harness, relays and hardware. The switch goes in a dash blank and plugs into accessory connectors already in the car. The bulb is **H11, 12 V 55 W**. The 2006-2007 kit (08V31-SDA-102) uses a different bumper, light trim and harnesses, but the **same switch part number**. **Installing either kit means cutting and drilling the front bumper**, which conflicts with the owner's no-drilling rule.

## Fog light switch part number, price, and other Honda switches that fit the blank

### Takeaway
The genuine fog switch is **08V31-SDA-1M035, "Switch, Foglight"**. It is catalogued for **2003-2007 Accord 4-door** (all trims) and the 2005-2007 Hybrid, and it is **discontinued**. The coupe uses 08V31-SDN-1M035. It has a 5-pin connector and pairs with switch harness 08V31-SDA-10030 (2003-2005). Its last dealer price was about $21.80. Used examples now run around $100 on eBay. The only other genuine Honda switches catalogued for this bank are the sunroof switch and the TCS Off switch. Using other Honda models' switches is community-only, with no fitment data.

### Inferences
- For a 2005 EX I4, the only genuine Honda switch that adds a working function in a blank is the **fog switch 08V31-SDA-1M035**, and it needs the fog kit to be useful.

## How the blank and lower panel come out

### Takeaway
Honda's procedure for the driver's dashboard lower cover: remove **one self-tapping screw**, release the **four clips on the left side first** by pulling the cover outward, release the **two clips on the right side last** using a flat-tip screwdriver twisted between clip and dash frame, then unplug the vehicle connector. The switch lid then pops out of the lower cover. A separate driver's under cover, if equipped, is held by three clips, one pin and one holder. No special tools are needed beyond a #2 Phillips and a small flat-tip screwdriver.

### Cited Findings
- [Honda install sheet] AII 24050 step 51: "Remove the one self-tapping screw. Release the four clips on the left side first by pulling out on the driver's dashboard lower cover. After releasing the four clips, insert the flat-tip screwdriver between the clip and the dashboard frame. Gently twist the screwdriver to release the clip. Release the two clips on the right side last. Unplug the vehicle connector, and remove the lower cover." The illustration labels "CLIPS (7)". Step 52: "If equipped, remove the driver's dashboard under cover (the three clips, one pin and one holder)." — [AII 24050](https://www.driveaccord.net/attachments/03-4drfogs-pdf.12405/)
- [Honda install sheet] Before starting, the sheet says to have the radio anti-theft code, record the presets, and **disconnect the negative battery cable**. After reconnecting, reset the driver's window AUTO function (hold the switch down until the window is fully open, then pull up until fully closed and hold 2 seconds). — [AII 24050](https://www.driveaccord.net/attachments/03-4drfogs-pdf.12405/)
- [Honda install sheet] The 2007 sheet AII 32672 uses the same "remove the switch lid from the driver's side dashboard lower cover" step. — [AII 32672](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/resources/installation/hpn/accord/2007-accord-4-door-fog-light-kit.pdf)
- [Community] The blanks "are able to pop out very easily" once the panel is removed. — [DriveAccord thread 309105](https://www.driveaccord.net/threads/3-blank-spots-under-left-vent-to-left-of-steering-wheel.309105/)
- [OEM catalog] Replacement clips if any break: 90666-SDA-A01 Clip, Snap Fitting, $0.61 per pack of 10. — [HondaPartsNow 2005 4dr EX garnish diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2005-honda-accord--4dr_ex-ka_5at/interior_bumper/instrument_panel_garnish_driver_side.html)

### Inferences
- Pulling the panel and swapping a blank is a 15-30 minute, reversible job, and the car stays drivable. This estimate is mine, not from a source. The battery disconnect is only needed if wiring is being connected. The owner has the radio code.
- A plastic trim tool is gentler on the clips than a bare screwdriver, but Honda's sheet uses a flat-tip.

### Gaps
- Whether a lid can be pushed out from the front without removing the lower cover. No source says.

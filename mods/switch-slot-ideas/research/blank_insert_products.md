Research date: 2026-10-26. Every price is the one shown on the listing or search snippet that day. Listings change often, so recheck before buying.
Labels follow the repo's CLAUDE.md tiers: everything here is retailer or seller copy, or forum (COMMUNITY) material. **Nothing here is a Tier 1-3 fitment confirmation.** The only reliable check is to measure the car's own blank (Tier 1) and look up the OEM part number in Honda's parts catalog by VIN (Tier 3).

## Q1. Which vendors make Honda-blank-sized inserts, and do Honda blanks of this era share one size?

### Takeaway
Honda blanks come in more than one size. The widely sold "Honda" 36x23 mm push-switch size is listed only for 2013+ Accords and 2014+ Civics, so it is very likely **not** the 7th-gen Accord size. I found **no** USB or voltmeter insert sold specifically for, and confirmed to fit, the 2003-2007 Accord blank. One seller groups the 2003-2007 Accord with the 2006-2011 Civic and 2009-2013 Fit as sharing a blank-mounted product, which suggests a common older blank size. That is seller copy only.

### Inferences
- The 7th-gen Accord blank is probably the older, larger Honda blank (shared with the 2006-2011 Civic and 2009-2013 Fit per Carzdeluxe), not the 36x23 mm size. Any "Honda universal" product sold as 36x23 mm or as fitting "2013+ Accord / 2014+ Civic" should be assumed **not** to fit until the owner measures. On AliExpress, the "Old Type" variant is the likely candidate, but no listing gave its dimensions.
- No established brand sells a 7th-gen-Accord-specific USB, voltmeter or 12V insert. The market is mostly unbranded AliExpress/Amazon sellers with loose fitment claims. That conflicts with the owner's "no no-name budget parts" rule, so any such insert would be an exception the owner has to choose knowingly.
- None of the products found here needs drilling into the dash if it truly matches the blank, because they snap into the blank opening. The risk is a near-miss in size, which forces trimming. Trimming a spare blank or bezel is acceptable under the owner's rules; trimming the car's own panel is not.

### Gaps

- **OEM part number for the 2003-2007 Accord blank: not found.** Forum part numbers 77755-TBA-A00ZA (2019 Accord), 77755-TF0-000ZA (Crosstour) and 77755-SNA-U00ZA (reported discontinued) are for other models. One search summary called SNA an "8th-gen Accord" number; SNA is more likely the 2006-2011 Civic platform code (UNVERIFIED, my memory). Free check: Honda's parts site by VIN, instrument panel / switch diagram. — [driveaccord part-number thread](https://www.driveaccord.net/threads/part-number-switch-blank.544901/); [driveaccord blank switch replacement](https://www.driveaccord.net/threads/blank-switch-replacement.567593/) (titles and snippets only; the threads themselves are behind a 402 gateway)
- Whether the 7th-gen Accord blanks match the 2002-2006 CR-V, 2003-2011 Element, 2005-2010 Odyssey and 2003-2008 Pilot: no source found either way.

### Inferences
- "Plug-and-play with the stock harness" only makes sense if the car already has a fog-light switch connector behind the blank. A 2005 Accord EX sedan without factory fogs probably has no such connector (UNVERIFIED). Free check: pull the blank and look for an unused connector, or read the fog-light accessory install instructions or the factory wiring diagram through the library's Chilton database.
- At $59.99, the ACM/FI unit costs about 4x the eBay/Amazon switch-with-harness items, and neither listing shows evidence that it is better made. The most likely genuine-quality route is a used or NOS Honda 08V31-SDA-1M035 switch (discontinued, so only salvage/eBay).
- A fog switch is only useful if fog lights are installed. As a plain "fill the blank" item, it adds nothing.

# Idea: hitch for extra storage (researched 2026-10-31)

Not bought. Owner asked what a hitch plus a hitch carrier would cost and whether it's smart.
Prices are web listings seen 2026-10-31 (some snapshots older); recheck before buying.

## The limit that decides everything

Honda's numbers (all VERIFIED, `specs.md` "Load and towing limits"): trailer max 1,000 lb,
tongue load about 10% of that (~100 lb), and the 850 lb max load includes tongue load.
The hitches below are rated 200 lb tongue weight, but the **car** is the lower limit.
A hitch cargo carrier hangs its whole weight on the hitch, 2–3 ft behind the bumper, so
it counts as tongue load. Treat **~100 lb for carrier + cargo** as the ceiling
(Claude's reading of OM p.190; Honda gives no direct carrier limit). A 300 or 500 lb
carrier rating is the basket's rating, not the car's.

## Options compared

- **Hitch + carrier:** ~$330–425 plus tax. Only ~100 lb minus the carrier's weight usable.
- **Hitch + wiring + ball mount + small trailer:** ~$800–1,050 plus tax, plus trailer
  registration (WA DOL, not checked). Up to 1,000 lb total with only ~100 lb on the hitch.
- **Hitch + bike rack:** fits the limit easily if bikes are the goal.

## Rules to remember
- WA: a rack or its cargo may block the plate only if the plate can still be read
  (RCW 46.16A.200, 2022 change; current text not re-read).
- OM lists towing as **severe service** (p.203 schedule). ATF is already overdue
  (`maintenance.md`); do it before any towing.
- Hitch bolt torque: from the hitch's install sheet. Not in this repo yet.

Accord tool tray: top layer

Only what this car's common jobs need (oil, spark plugs, wheels, quick checks). Everything else goes in a lower layer.

Have Add Drawn for a ~7 × 17 in foam (estimated from your photo). Not a cutting template.

Why this layout

  • Grouped by job, in reach order. Ratchet and torque adapter at the top, the sockets you use with them right below.
  • Spark plug kit together: plug socket, both solid extensions, gap gauge, torque adapter (13 lbf·ft, Chilton).
  • Oil change kit together: drain plug socket, filter cap wrench, torque adapter (33 lbf·ft, OM p.215).
  • Wheel kit together: lug socket, lock key. Break lugs loose with the 1/2 breaker bar, not the 3/8 ratchet plus adapter.
  • 11, 13 and 15 mm moved down. Hondas mostly use 10, 12, 14, 17 and 19 (general pattern, unverified for every bolt on this car). Move them back up if a job proves otherwise.

Layer 2: full socket set

  • Shallow 8, 9, 11, 13, 15, 16, 18, 19 11/13/15 moved here
  • Deep 12, 14, 17 add
  • 10 in extension, wobble extension, U-joint add (never on spark plugs)
  • Metric hex bit sockets add

Layer 3: hand tools & checks

  • Combination wrenches 10–19 mm
  • 1/4 in drive set (under-dash, console screws)
  • Test light, fuse puller, spare fuses
  • Telescoping magnet, inspection mirror, tire pressure gauge, headlamp

Separate long box

  • 1/2 in breaker bar add, EverTough torque wrench, 18 in pry bar, dead blow, 32 mm axle socket

Separate wiring kit (mods)

  • Soldering iron, solder, heat-shrink, heat gun, stripper, add-a-circuit fuse taps, Posi-Taps, zip ties

Cutting tips

  • Lay every tool out and trace it before cutting anything.
  • Cut a thumb notch beside each socket so it lifts out easily.
  • Write the real size in the foam (e.g. "17" next to the drain plug socket).
  • An empty cutout = a tool still in the engine bay. Check before closing the hood.

To confirm: drain plug and lug socket sizes (stamped on the socket), Quinn adapter range (back label), drive sizes of the ratchet and adapters, and whether the car has locking lug nuts.

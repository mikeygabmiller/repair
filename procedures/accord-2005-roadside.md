# Roadside: flat tire, jump start, overheating, warning lights, towing (2005 Accord EX-L 2.4 L AT)

Sources: Honda owner's manual (OM) "Taking Care of the Unexpected", pp.245–264 (page images
read 2026-11-10). Every step below is from those pages unless labeled otherwise.
It's the only car: keep the spare aired up (60 psi), jumper cables or a jump pack, and a
flashlight in it.

## Flat tire (OM pp.246–251)

**Time:** ~20–30 min (estimate). **Leaves the car drivable** on the spare: **50 mph max**
(OM p.246), harsher ride, less grip; get the real tire fixed or replaced soon.

1. Off the road onto firm, level, non-slippery ground, well away from traffic. P,
   parking brake on, hazards on, ignition to LOCK (0), everyone out (OM p.247).
2. Trunk floor up (lift the back edge); take out the tool kit; unscrew the wing bolt;
   lift out the spare (OM p.247).
3. Jack: behind the cover in the **right** side of the trunk; turn the handle
   counterclockwise, pull the cover. Turn the jack's end bracket counterclockwise to free it
   (OM p.248).
4. **Loosen each wheel nut 1/2 turn** while the tire is still on the ground (OM p.248).
5. Jack under the **jacking point** nearest the flat (the notched pinch-weld point just
   behind the front wheel / ahead of the rear wheel, picture OM p.248). Wind it up until
   the jack head touches; **the jacking-point tab must sit in the jack's notch**.
6. Raise with the extension + wheel wrench until the flat is off the ground (OM p.249).
   **Never put any part of your body under the car on this jack** (OM p.247 warning).
7. Nuts off, flat off, lay it face-up. Wipe the hub and the spare's mounting face clean
   (careful: hub may be hot) (OM p.249).
8. Spare on, nuts finger-tight, then snug in a **crisscross** until the wheel sits flat
   against the hub. Don't fully tighten in the air (OM p.250).
9. Lower, remove the jack, tighten the nuts crisscross: **80 lbf·ft (108 N·m)** (OM p.250).
   No torque wrench on the roadside: tighten firmly with the wheel wrench and **have them
   torqued at the next shop/tire store** (OM p.250 says the same).
10. Stow the flat face-down in the well; flip the spacer cone on the wing bolt to the
    "normal tire" side; screw it down. Store and lock the jack (OM p.250). Loose items in a
    crash hurt people (OM p.251).

**COMMUNITY tip:** carry a cheap 1/2-in breaker bar + 19 mm or 21 mm socket (check your lug
size before buying: not in the manual) and a 12 V tire inflator. Why: the factory wrench is
short, and a slow leak (like the bead leak in open issue 11) is often fixable by airing up
and driving to a tire shop instead of changing the wheel at all.

**Plugging a tire:** only a tread puncture. Never a sidewall, bead or shoulder leak (that's
a dismount-and-inspect job). Honda's bulletin **A06-082 "Tubeless Tire Repair Information"**
exists (Chilton bulletin list); not read in full.

## Engine won't start (OM pp.251–252)

| What you hear | Check | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing, clicks, or slow crank | In P or N? Key ON, headlights on: very dim or dead = battery | Clean/tighten battery terminals (OM p.242), then jump start |
| Headlights fine but dim/go out when cranking | Battery discharged or corroded connections | Terminals, then jump |
| Headlights don't dim at all, starter silent | Fuses (OM pp.265–269) | If fuses OK: starter/ignition circuit, tow |
| Starter spins normally, engine won't run | Right starting method (OM p.175)? Immobilizer light blinking rapidly = key not recognised (OM p.77)? Fuel? | Fuses (fuel pump: interior #19 IG Fuel Pump 15 A). Still nothing: tow |

Car-specific: recall **07V097000** (fuel pump relay) and **05V132000** (fuse box terminal) were
both no-start/stall causes on this model. NHTSA shows neither open on this VIN
(`profile.md`). Open issue 2 (P0685, main relay circuit) is the same family of fault.

## Jump start (OM pp.253–255)

1. Look at the battery first. **Frozen or slushy electrolyte: don't jump it** (it can burst).
2. Everything electrical off; P; parking brake on.
3. Cable 1: **+ on this car → + on the booster**.
4. Cable 2: **− on the booster → the engine grounding strap** shown in the OM p.254 picture
   (4-cylinder). **Not** this car's battery negative, not any other engine part.
5. Booster car running at fast idle. Start this car. Slow cranking: check clamp contact.
6. Remove in reverse: − from this car, − from booster, + from this car, + from booster.
   Keep the clamps apart.
7. Afterwards: a free battery and charging test at a parts store (open issue 6).

Push-starting an automatic is impossible (OM p.253).

## Overheating (OM pp.256–257)

1. Pull over, P, parking brake, A/C and accessories off, hazards on.
2. **Steam or spray:** engine off, **don't open the hood** until it stops.
3. **No steam:** leave it idling and watch the gauge; it should drop fast if it was just
   load. Back to the middle → carry on.
4. Stays at red → engine off. Look for an obvious leak (split hose). Leak = tow.
5. No leak: check the reserve tank, add to MIN–MAX. Empty tank: let the gauge come down to
   the middle or lower, then (cloth over the cap) turn the radiator cap counterclockwise
   **without pushing** to the first stop to vent, then push and turn to remove.
6. Engine running, climate on max heat/FULL AUTO 90 °F, top the radiator to the base of the
   neck. Plain water only if that's all you have, then drain and refill properly soon.
7. Cap on tight; watch the gauge. Back to red = tow.

**Never open the radiator cap hot** (OM pp.217, 257). Driving at red damages the engine.

## Warning lights while driving

| Light | Meaning | Do this | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oil pressure (oil can) | Pressure very low or gone | **Pull off and shut off now.** Wait a minute, check dipstick, top up, restart: if it doesn't go out within 10 s, tow | OM p.258 |
| Charging (battery) | Not charging | All electrical off, **keep the engine running** (restarting drains it fast), drive straight to help | OM p.259 |
| Check engine (MIL) steady | Emissions fault | After a fill-up: check the gas cap (takes 3+ days to clear). Otherwise get the code read soon (free at many parts stores). **Flashing = misfire: slow down, get it checked now** (COMMUNITY, general OBD-II) | OM p.260 |
| BRAKE | Low brake fluid (parking brake off) | Pedal normal: check the level at the next stop. Pedal sinks or soft: stop, tow | OM p.261 |
| ABS | Anti-lock off, normal brakes still work | Open issue 4. With BRAKE + ABS both on: Honda dealer now | OM p.261 |
| SRS | Airbag system fault | Dealer or SRS shop only (open issue 1). Never DIY | — |

## Towing (OM pp.263–264)

- **Flatbed** is best. **Wheel-lift** is acceptable.
- Front wheels on the ground only if damage forces it: parking brake off, start the engine,
  shift to **D then N**, engine off. No farther than **50 miles** and under **35 mph**.
  Can't start it or shift it → **front wheels must be off the ground** (or the
  transmission is damaged).
- Never tow with rope/chain; never lift by the bumpers. Four-wheels-down behind another
  vehicle needs a proper tow bar and ACCESSORY (I) so the wheel doesn't lock.

## Moonroof won't close (OM p.262)

Check the moonroof fuse (interior #28). Still stuck: moonroof wrench from the trunk tool
kit; pry the round plug out of the headliner centre; turn the wrench in the socket until
closed; refit the plug. Then have it looked at.

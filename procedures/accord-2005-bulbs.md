# Exterior bulbs: 2005 Accord EX-L sedan, 2.4 L

Sources: Honda owner's manual (OM) "Lights", pp.226–232 (page images read 2026-11-10);
bulb ratings OM p.275 (`specs.md`).
Difficulty: easy. Time: 5–20 min per bulb (estimate). Car stays drivable.
Tools: flat screwdriver (fender clips), nothing else per the manual.

## Before you start
- Buy the bulb by reading the old one (trade number printed on its base) or from the
  rating table in `quick-reference.md`. Headlights: high **HB3 (9005)**, low **HB4 (9006)**
  (VERIFIED: OM p.275).
- Halogen glass: **don't touch it**. Handle by the plastic base; if touched, clean with
  denatured alcohol. Oil, sweat or a scratch makes it overheat and shatter (OM p.226).
- Headlight aim was set at the factory; heavy loads or a trailer can need re-aiming by a
  shop (OM p.226).
- Upgrades (LED/HID) are in `mods/lighting-upgrades.md`: read that first. Swapping in a
  non-DOT LED in a halogen housing is a resale and legality problem.

## High beam (OM pp.227–228)
1. Hood open. **Passenger side only:** undo the two fasteners and lift off the air intake
   cover.
2. Squeeze the connector tab, slide the connector off the bulb.
3. Turn the bulb 1/4 turn counterclockwise, pull it out.
4. New bulb in, 1/4 turn clockwise to lock. Connector on.
5. Headlights on to test. Passenger side: refit the cover; push the fastener heads until
   they lock.

## Low beam (through the wheel well) (OM pp.228–229)
1. **Passenger side bulb:** start the engine, steer full **left**, engine off. **Driver side
   bulb:** steer full **right**.
2. Flat screwdriver: pop the two holding clips out of the inner fender liner.
3. Pull the liner back away from the fender and bumper.
4. Bulb 1/4 turn counterclockwise, out. Squeeze connector tab, slide it off.
5. Reverse. Test. Liner back, clips in, push each centre to lock.

## Front side marker / turn signal / parking (OM pp.229–230)
Same wheel-well access as the low beam (steer, two clips, pull the liner back). Socket 1/4
turn counterclockwise out of the headlight assembly. Bulb: push in and turn
counterclockwise. New bulb, socket back in clockwise, test, liner and clips back.

## Rear bulbs in the fenders (OM p.230)
Trunk open → pull the light assembly cover off by its outside edge → socket 1/4 turn
counterclockwise → bulb pulls straight out → new bulb pushed straight in until it bottoms →
socket back clockwise → test → cover back.

## Rear bulbs in the trunk lid (OM p.231)
Trunk open. (The manual's trim-clip removal step is marked for the **6-cylinder** only.)
Bend back the corner of the trunk-lid trim to expose the sockets → socket 1/4 turn
counterclockwise → bulb pulls straight out → new bulb in, socket back → test → trim back,
clips pressed in. A trim clip that breaks: a dealer or parts store sells them cheap; don't
glue.

## High-mount brake light (OM p.232)
Trunk open; under the rear shelf, socket 1/4 turn counterclockwise → bulb pulls straight
out → new bulb, socket back.

## Interior bulbs
Not covered by the manual's steps. Dome bulb confirmed as a 31 mm festoon, 8 W (owner,
2026-10-26, `parts.md`); map lights and trunk bulb not yet read. The middle courtesy light
dims with the instrument-panel brightness, so an LED there may flicker (OM p.101,
`specs.md`).

## What to ask a shop
"Valve clearance inspection, cold engine, adjust only out-of-spec valves to Honda's 2.4 L
K24A4 spec, new valve cover gasket and spark plug tube seals, tell me which valves were out
and by how much." Ask which spec they used and write it here. Time at a shop: a few hours
(estimate); off the road for the day.

## Why it matters (COMMUNITY reasoning, UNVERIFIED for this engine)
Exhaust valves on high-mileage engines tend to **tighten**, not loosen; a tight valve doesn't
tick, it quietly stops sealing fully and can burn. So "adjust only if noisy" doesn't catch the
risky case. That's why an inspection past 110k is worth paying for.

## DIY outline (only after the spec is settled)
Cold engine (TSX article: head under 100 °F). Valve cover off (coils out first,
`accord-2005-spark-plugs.md`). Rotate the crank to each cylinder's TDC on compression (crank
pulley bolt, clockwise). Feeler gauge between rocker and valve tip; adjust with the locknut
and screw; torque the locknut. New gasket and tube seals; cover bolts to the 2.4 L torque
and sequence (**not found yet**, checklist 32).

What the labels mean: VERIFIED from the owner's manual (OM) or Chilton · COMMUNITY forum/YouTube tip · UNVERIFIED not confirmed for this car.

The car is undrivable only from pulling the plug until the refill is done (~30 min). Don't start this the night before a work day.

Before you start: have all of this

Skip: pan-type filter kits, pan gaskets and additives (Shudder Fixx etc.). Honda's fluid change uses only the drain plug and the dipstick tube. The small inline filter under the fuse box is a separate job: don't touch it this round. OM pp.219–220, Chilton

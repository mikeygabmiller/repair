# Winter prep: 2005 Accord EX-L 2.4 L AT (only car, Washington winters)

Sources: OM pp.216–217 (washer fluid, coolant), p.225 (hood latch), pp.237–241 (tires,
snow tires, chains), pp.242–243 (battery), p.253 (frozen battery), p.199 (severe schedule
conditions). Read 2026-11-10. Items without an OM page are labeled.
Time: ~1 h of checks, plus whatever they turn up. Car stays drivable.

| # | Check | What to do | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Coolant** | Honda Type 2 premixed 50/50 gives freeze protection without mixing; never top up with plain water in winter. Overdue for a change (`maintenance.md`): look at it cold; murky or low → do the change before a hard freeze | OM p.217; change: `procedures/accord-2005-brake-fluid-coolant.md` |
| 2 | **Washer fluid** | Winter-rated commercial washer fluid only. **No engine antifreeze** (damages paint), no vinegar/water (damages the pump). Run summer fluid low first so it doesn't dilute | OM p.216 |
| 3 | **Battery** | Free load test at a parts store before the first cold snap (cold cuts cranking power; COMMUNITY, general). Terminals clean and tight (open issue 2). **Never jump a frozen battery** (slushy/frozen electrolyte can burst) | OM pp.242, 253 |
| 4 | **Tires** | Check pressures more often: cold air lowers the reading (COMMUNITY: roughly 1 psi per 10 °F). Set to 32/30 **cold** (OM p.237). Tread: replace at 3+ wear bars (OM p.238). **The left front bead leak (open issue 11) must be fixed before winter** | OM pp.236–238 |
| 5 | **Snow tires (if bought)** | Radial, **same size and load range** as the originals (P205/60R16 91V), **on all four wheels**. Their dry-road grip can be lower; ask the dealer about speed rating | OM p.240 |
| 6 | **Chains (EX)** | Cable type only: **SCC Radial Chain CH2311T or SCC SC Cable SC1030** (limited clearance on this car). Front tires only, as tight as possible, slow; stop if they hit the body. Never on the compact spare | OM pp.241, 246 |
| 7 | **Wipers** | Replace if streaking (blades are from 2026-09). Lift the arms off the glass in an ice storm; don't open the hood with them raised | OM pp.234–235 |
| 8 | **Hood latch** | Clean and grease the latch pivots (sticks in freezing rain) | OM p.225 |
| 9 | **Lights** | All working (dark commutes): `procedures/accord-2005-routine-checks.md`. Aim if they light the trees, not the road | OM pp.201, 226 |
| 10 | **Rust** | Rinse the underbody every couple of weeks when roads are de-iced. De-iced roads also put the car on the **severe** maintenance schedule | `plans/long-term-ownership.md`; OM p.199 |
| 11 | **Emergency kit** | Jumper cables or a jump pack, flashlight, tire inflator, gloves, blanket, ice scraper, the spare at 60 psi | COMMUNITY; spare pressure OM p.246 |
| 12 | **Door seals** | A silicone spray on the door and trunk seals keeps them from freezing shut | OM p.244 (storage tips) |

**Driving notes for this car this winter:** ABS is not working until the bearing is replaced
(open issue 4): in a skid, braking can lock the front tires, and locked front tires don't
steer (UNVERIFIED reasoning: general vehicle dynamics; it's how the ditch slide happened).
Leave more room, brake earlier and gently, and get item 3 of `next-steps.md` done first.

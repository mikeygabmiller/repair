## F03 — Brake pad and rotor replacement, front and rear

Paths: Brakes > Front Disc Brakes > Brake Pads / Brake Disc (Rotor) > Removal & Installation; Brakes > Rear Disc Brakes > Brake Pads / Brake Disc (Rotor) > Removal & Installation
No torque values and no tool list were shown in these steps. Brake disc specification values are NOT FOUND for the 2.4 L (Brake disc Specifications lists Hybrid/V6 only).

Front pads, summarized:
1. Check section precautions; remove/discarding brake fluid from the master cylinder as needed.
2. Raise and support, remove front wheels, remove brake hose bracket bolt and caliper flange bolt.
3. Pivot the caliper up, remove pads and shims, remove pad retainers, check caliper pins move freely.
4. On install, apply a thin coat of M-77 assembly paste (part number shown in Chilton as o8798-9010) to the pad sides of the shims and the backs of the pads, wipe off excess, reverse the removal, check/refill the master cylinder.
Warning in the article: asbestos fibers in brake dust — do not sand/grind lining unless equipment contains dust, do not clean with compressed air or dry brushing, dampen and wipe, dispose of cloth/residue in a labelled impermeable container, follow OSHA/EPA practices. Front figure is captioned for an Accord V6.

Front rotor, summarized:
1. Siphon about half the fluid from the master cylinder.
2. Raise/support, remove wheel, remove caliper bolts and lift the caliper away; hang it on wire — never let the hose carry its weight.
3. Remove bracket bolts and bracket, then rotor retaining screws.
4. Pull the rotor straight off the studs, or if seized, thread in two 8 x 1.25 mm bolts, two turns at a time, until it releases.
5. On install, retract the piston with a large C-clamp if needed, fit rotor and screws, bracket and bolts, caliper and pads, fill the master cylinder, fit the wheel, pump the pedal until pads seat and the pedal is firm — do not move the vehicle until a firm pedal is obtained — then road test.

Rear pads, summarized:
1. Check precautions; remove/discarding fluid as needed; raise/support, remove rear wheels, release the parking brake.
2. Remove caliper bolts and caliper, then shims and pads; remove retainers, check pins.
3. On install, apply M-77 paste as above, fit pads/shims, rotate the caliper piston clockwise into the cylinder and align its cutout with the inner pad tab, lubricating the boot with rubber grease so it does not twist.
4. Reverse the removal, press the pedal several times, road test, check parking brake adjustment, check/refill the master cylinder. Note: pedal effort may be greater immediately after a pad set change.
Rear rotor: body text reads identically to the front rotor procedure, steps 1–15, including the 8 x 1.25 mm bolt release method and the firm-pedal caution.

## F04 — Spark plug replacement

Path: Engine Electrical > Ignition System > Spark Plugs > Removal & Installation
The R&I body shows no applicable-vehicles note, so it is reported as Chilton's general article under this title, not claimed as 2.4 L-specific. Spark Plug Inspection and Specifications articles list TSX/Hybrid/V6 only.
Summarized:
1. Disconnect the negative battery cable, note the radio security code, let a hot engine cool fully.
2. Remove the intake manifold cover and the trim covers above the cylinder head covers.
3. Label and disconnect each coil connector, remove coil fasteners and coils (coil-over-plug: each plug has its own coil above it).
4. Remove plugs with a spark plug socket with a rubber insert, turning counterclockwise. Warning: do not use a flexible extension — shear force can break a plug in the head.
5. Inspect coils for damage/deterioration and debris/oil; replace a damaged coil.
6. Check the gap with a wire feeler gauge — do not adjust it in this article's wording. Gap shown: 0.039-0.043 in. (1.0-1.1 mm); replace a plug if the gap exceeds 0.051 in. (1.3 mm). Torque on install: 13 ft. lbs. (18 Nm). These match the A–E values already recorded.
7. Apply a light coat of anti-seize to the threads, thread plugs in by hand (a short piece of rubber hose on the plug can help in tight spots), never force them — cross-threading risk.
8. Refit coils, connectors, covers, reconnect the battery and enter the radio code.
One warning in the article is explicitly about the 3.5 L V6 platinum plugs and gap adjustment — not 2.4 L data.

## F08 — Front and rear struts

NOT FOUND in usable 2.4 L form. The procedures found are Hybrid-only, with no numeric torques in the prose — only "specified torque value(s)".
Front, Hybrid-only (Suspension > Front Suspension > Struts > Front Damper/Spring Removal and Installation, 2005-07 Accord Hybrid), summarized: raise and support the front, remove the wheel, remove the damper fork from the damper and lower arm, remove the two 8 mm and three 10 mm flange nuts from the damper top and remove the assembly. On install, align the damper tab inward, loosely fit 8 mm nuts and new 10 mm nuts, fit the fork over the driveshaft and lower arm, align the damper tab with the fork slot, loosely fit the pinch bolt, fit a new fork mounting bolt/nut and lightly tighten, load the suspension with a floor jack under the lower arm, tighten top flange nuts, pinch bolts and fork mounting nut to the specified values, clean disc/wheel mating surfaces, fit the wheel, check alignment.
Front damper/spring disassembly article: heading present, body blank on re-check.
Rear, Hybrid-only (Suspension > Rear Suspension > Struts > Strut Removal and Installation, body headed Rear Damper/Spring Removal and Installation, 2005-07 Accord Hybrid), summarized: remove the rear seat-back and two flange nuts, raise/support, remove the wheel, remove the lower mounting bolt from the knuckle, disconnect the stabilizer link (hold the pin with a hex wrench), lower the suspension and remove the damper — mark left/right springs, they differ. On install, loosely fit new top flange nuts, new lower bolt and new stabilizer flange nut, load the suspension with a jack, tighten lower bolt and stabilizer nut to the specified values, fit the wheel, tighten top nuts to the specified value, check alignment, refit the seat-back.
No strut figure torque was legibly verified, so none is reported.

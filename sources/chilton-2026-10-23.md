# Chilton Library + parts catalog + NHTSA lookups, 2026-10-23

Run by Claude in Chrome for the owner. Two reports: **Report 1** (ABS-light questions)
and **Report 2** (a broader spec sweep). Values below are copied from those reports;
quotes are Chilton's wording as the reports gave it. Screenshot numbers/IDs refer to the
browser session; no image files were saved to this repo.

Access: Obion County Public Library entry to Chilton Library (Gale),
`http://infotrac.galegroup.com/default/tel_p_obioncpl?db=CHLL`. Chilton is a single-page
app, so every article has the same URL (`https://app.chiltonlibrary.com/search/repair`);
the menu path is the citation.

**Big caveat (both reports):** for this car, many Chilton articles exist only in
"V6 Engine", "Accord Hybrid" or "Acura TSX (K24)" versions, with no 2.4 L Accord version.
The "Applies to" column says which. A value from another version is **not** confirmed
for the 2.4 L.

Before any public release: this file quotes copyrighted Chilton text. Keep the paths,
drop the quotes (see `CLAUDE.md`).

### Procedures (Report 2's summaries)

**A02 Front halfshaft R&I** (article covers this vehicle, M/T and A/T; i61, i62): raise and
support, wheels off; raise the locking tab, remove spindle nut, drain transaxle; hold the
stabilizer pin with a hex wrench, remove the flange nut, separate link from lower arm;
remove ball joint lock pin and castle nut, separate ball joint from lower arm; pull the
knuckle out and free the outboard joint from the hub with a plastic hammer; on A/T
remove the heat shield; pry the right inboard joint out of the differential with a
prybar. Reverse to install; turn the wheel by hand to check interference, refill the
transaxle, check alignment. **Don't pull on the halfshaft** (inboard joint can come
apart); pull straight out to protect the oil seal. No spindle nut torque in this summary.

**A03 Front hub/bearing** (**Hybrid**; i71-i73): tools hub dis/assembly tool 42 mm
07GAF-SD40100, ball joint remover 28 mm 07MAC-SL0A202, attachments 07746-0010600 and
07GAD-SD40101, driver 07749-0010000, support base 07965-SD90100, thread protector
07AAF-SDAA100, hydraulic press. Raise the stake, remove spindle nut, remove wheel sensor
and brake hose bracket; cotter pins and castle nuts off tie rod and lower ball joint;
separate hub from driveshaft, remove knuckle; press hub out, press bearing out; press new
bearing in, **brown magnetic encoder side inward**. New spindle nut, new cotter pins.

**A04 Lower ball joint** (i81, i74, i109-i111): cotter pin and nut off, ball joint removal
tool on the pin threads, never strike the joint. No press-in procedure stated.

**C01 ATF change** (i448-i456): warm up (fan comes on), level ground, engine off, drain,
new washer on drain plug, refill Honda ATF-Z1 to the upper mark, new washer on filler
bolt. Level check within 60-90 seconds of shutdown, dipstick cap arrow facing forward.

**C03 Coolant** (**V6** article): heater to max, drain, fill reserve to MAX and radiator to
the filler neck base, cap loose, run until the fan cycles at least twice, top up, cap
tight, check for leaks.

**C04 Brake bleeding**: clean Honda DOT 3 from an unopened container; FL, FR, RR, RL.

**D04 Idle learn** (**V6 M/T** article): all loads off; reset ECM/PCM with HDS; key ON (II)
2 s; start, hold 3,000 rpm in P/N until the fan comes on; idle 5 min throttle closed
(fan time doesn't count). Needed after ECM reset or throttle body cleaning, not after
just erasing DTCs.

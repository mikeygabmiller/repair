### Rotor retaining screws
Present, per Brakes > Front Disc Brakes > Brake Disc (Rotor) > Removal & Installation (G18). Procedure, paraphrased, with the asbestos caution summarised: brake dust may contain asbestos; do not sand/grind lining or clean parts with compressed air or dry brushing; dampen with a fine water mist, wipe with a damp cloth, dispose of cloth/residue in a labelled impermeable container, and follow OSHA/EPA practices. Steps: remove about half the fluid from the master cylinder (siphon or turkey baster); raise and support the vehicle, remove the wheel; remove caliper mounting bolts, lift the caliper away and hang it by wire so its weight is not on the flex hose; remove bracket bolts and bracket; remove the rotor retaining screws; lift the rotor straight off the studs, or if seized thread in two 8 x 1.25 mm bolts, two turns at a time, until released; on installation, retract the piston with a large C-clamp or suitable tool if needed; fit the rotor and tighten the mounting screws; refit bracket and caliper and mounting bolts; fill the master cylinder to the proper level with fresh fluid; refit the wheel and lower the vehicle; pump the pedal until pads are seated and a firm pedal is achieved before moving the vehicle; road test. **Retaining-screw torque: NOT FOUND in this article's text.**

Notes, paraphrased: if the ABS modulator has been opened or replaced, it may be necessary to bleed each line at the modulator unit; air can be trapped far upstream, so use a fast flow of a large volume of fluid; manual bleeding needs an assistant unless a self-bleeding tool is available; avoid spilling fluid on paint (wash with water immediately if spilled); if the reservoir runs dry, restart from the first fitting; remove old fluid and clean the reservoir with a clean lint-free cloth; bleed at each fitting until no air bubbles, or a minimum of 4–5 times per bleeder; attach a clear hose to the bleeder into a jar with enough fresh fluid to submerge the hose end; assistant pumps 3–4 times and holds pressure, open the bleeder at least 1/4 turn, close it before the assistant slowly releases the pedal; keep topping off the master cylinder and never let it run dry; test-drive to confirm firm pedal. **No scan-tool requirement for ABS bleeding is stated in this article's text; the only ABS-related bleeding note is the modulator-opened/replaced note above.**
